# Continuity conflicts and resolutions

1. **Two days vs. three-day ride.** He hasn't reached the pass — he's at the outer picket, a day short, or he rode through the night on a remount. Day three still matters.
2. **Sixty vs. forty soldiers.** Twenty aren't garrison: a relief column, or armed smugglers camped inside — which explains the ledger and the rot.
3. **Snow falling vs. enemy after first snow.** Make it a false first snow: flurries that don't settle, so the trigger hasn't fired. Ilan reads it as a countdown.
4. **Mira showing the smuggler's ledger.** She shows a quartermaster's manifest she believes routine; Ilan spots the false entries. Her ignorance survives and becomes dramatic. Route knowledge is local, not criminal.
5. **Gripping the rope.** He can't: he wraps it round his right forearm and waist, hauls with body weight, left arm hooked over the ledge. The injury costs him visibly.
