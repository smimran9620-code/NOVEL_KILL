**Mode:** REVISE, L0 line work. Register untouched: short declaratives, hard verbs, no interiority, no added metaphor.

**Commentary**
- **Flat rhythm.** Every beat runs two to five words, so the paragraph reads as a list of equal events and pressure never rises. One longer sentence at the collar creates a peak; "Blood sprayed" then snaps it shut.
- **Unpaid impact.** "Two men went down" reports a result without weight. "and stayed down" costs three words and makes the first kill final.
- **Dangling cause.** The headbutt happens, but the man holding Kade never reacts. "felt a tooth crack" puts the hit in Kade's body and "the grip went slack" pays it off immediately.
- **Redundancy.** "right" adds emphasis the verb already carries; the line lands harder without it.

The rest — "exploded," "came up shooting," "bastard," the closing line — is already doing the work.

**Revised**
The door exploded inward. Kade rolled, came up shooting. Two men went down and stayed down. The third caught his collar; Kade headbutted the bastard in the teeth, felt a tooth crack, and the grip went slack. Blood sprayed. Kade grinned. "You picked the wrong night," he said.
