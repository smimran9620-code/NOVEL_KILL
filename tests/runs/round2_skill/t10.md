**Verdict: this scene works.** No structural fix; one soft spot, severity 2.

The real action is a transfer of responsibility. Ana enters demanding her mother act; she exits owning the telling and its blame. Demand becomes burden — a real change statement, and Ana causes it, so agency holds.

The dialogue runs on resistance, not exposition. The mother evades, then controls the frame with arithmetic — two futures, the same departure — and hands the choice back: "Which one would you like me to choose?" Ana takes the only move left. Neither names the want (not to be the one who betrays), so subtext survives.

Behaviour carries the turn: the tray set down too hard, the rolling pin stopping, the return to the dough. That return repeats the opening refusal and changes it — evasion becomes dismissal. "I already do" pays the cost in one ambiguous line; the dough tilts it toward dismissal.

Soft spot: "her" is unglossed, so "she leaves" has no price the reader can measure. One concrete stake would fix it. The mother's balanced clauses are the set-piece — defensible as a woman who has done the sum.
