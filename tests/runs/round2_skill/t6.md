Mode: DIAGNOSE — five conflicts, each resolved by cause and cost.

1. Travel: two days vs a three-day ride. Ilan rides ahead of the column through the night and ruins his horse. Cost: he arrives alone and spent; the column is a day behind when the snow comes.

2. Garrison: sixty counted vs forty. The extra twenty are levy drovers, mustered and fed from fort stores — new fact, not a renumber, and it seeds the pay discrepancy.

3. Hand: he grips the rope though his left hand cannot. It is belayed from above and he is hauled up in front of the men. Cost: visible dependence, lost authority.

4. Mira: she shows the smuggler's ledger and explains the route, knowing nothing. She has kept the quartermaster's manifest and reads it as supply accounts — a supply road, she thinks. Only the numbers make it a smuggling ledger: forty paid, sixty fed. Ilan sees it, tells her nothing. Her ignorance survives as dramatic irony.

5. Snow: falling already, though the enemy comes after first snow. It falls at the pass, not at the fort — altitude. The window is open while he is still ahead of the warning.
