# Chapter Review Checklist

Use at chapter level, after scene audits. The chapter is a unit of orientation and pressure, not a container for scenes.

## 1. Chapter question

- [ ] What question does this chapter raise or press? ______
- [ ] Is it answered, complicated, or deferred by the end?
- [ ] A chapter that raises nothing is a transition; keep those short and few.

## 2. Causal chain

List the chapter's beats as "because -> therefore -> but":

1. ______
2. ______
3. ______

- [ ] No beat is joined only by "and then".
- [ ] The final beat is caused by a decision made inside the chapter, not delivered from outside.

## 3. Entry and exit

- [ ] Entry: begins inside a situation, not with recap or weather.
- [ ] Exit: ends on a decision, consequence, or new pressure, not on withheld information.
- [ ] The exit changes the terms of the next chapter rather than merely hiding them.
- [ ] No fake-out that the next chapter simply denies.

## 4. Information order

- [ ] What the reader learns, in order: ______
- [ ] Is anything learned before it can matter? Move it later.
- [ ] Is anything learned after the reader needed it? Move it earlier.

## 5. POV discipline

- [ ] Only the POV's perceptions and inferences are asserted as fact.
- [ ] Other characters' interiors are inferred, not narrated, unless the POV convention allows it.
- [ ] The POV is not a passive recipient for more than one scene in a row.

- Knowledge:
- Relationship:
- Stakes:
- Position/possession:
- Self-understanding:

At least one must change. If only knowledge changes, confirm it is knowledge the reader did not already have.

## 7. Scene variety

Track the last five chapters' dominant scene types. If fewer than three types, vary the next chapter deliberately. Types: negotiation, pursuit, discovery, ritual, failure, aftermath, preparation, performance, interrogation, gift, institutional procedure.

## 8. Repetition scan

- [ ] Repeated sentence openings in this chapter: ______
- [ ] Repeated image or metaphor: ______
- [ ] Restated information from prior chapters: ______
- [ ] Any repetition that is deliberate (refrain, escalation)? If so, how does it change?

## 9. Continuity spot-check

- [ ] Names and spellings match the ledger.
- [ ] Injuries, possessions, location, time of day consistent with prior chapter.
- [ ] Promises made are logged; promises paid are marked.

- Fails because: ______
- Minimal fix: ______
- Level of fix (L0-L4): ______
